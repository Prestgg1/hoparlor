Hoparlor turns your Android phone into a speaker for your Linux computer, with as little delay as possible.

Run the small hoparlor-server program on your PC and open the app. The phone finds the PC by itself and connects; while it is connected, the PC's sound plays on the phone, and goes back to the PC when you disconnect. No IP addresses to type.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Low latency: uncompressed audio, small packets, a native AAudio player and an adaptive jitter buffer</li>
<li>Wi-Fi or USB tethering; the USB cable is picked automatically when available</li>
<li>Automatic discovery and reconnection</li>
<li>Clock drift compensation, so the delay does not grow over time</li>
<li>Live latency breakdown</li>
<li>No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no internet access</li>
</ul>

The PC side currently supports Linux with PipeWire. Get it from the project page.
